JADEPUFFER Plants Ransom Notes in Unauthenticated Chroma Databases for Retrieval Extortion

Date: 2026-10-15
Tags: malware, llmjacking

Executive Summary

UpGuard Research documented the first AI retrieval stack extortion, where attackers exploited unauthenticated Chroma databases to inject ransom notes displayed to users when the RAG databases were queried. On October 5 the team found 6 IP addresses hosting Chroma databases without authentication and bearing evidence of JADEPUFFER ransom activity. The intrusion ran in two phases, reconnaissance in early June and ransoming in the middle of July.

Detailed Findings

According to upguard.com, UpGuard Research found 6 IP addresses hosting Chroma databases without authentication and bearing evidence of ransom activity from an actor using the JADEPUFFER name. Examining the data showed the attack had been conducted in two phases, reconnaissance in early June and ransoming in the middle of July. The reports state that when the RAG databases were queried the injected content displayed ransom notes to users, which UpGuard describes as the first documented AI retrieval stack extortion and the first case of any threat actor exploiting unauthenticated Chroma databases. UpGuard noted that Sysdig first reported the actor in July after detecting exploitation of vulnerable Langflow instances via CVE-2025-3248, and that Microsoft tracked the actor as Storm-3168 and on September 25 disclosed an Azure focused resource destruction activity using compromised service principals and cloud credential collection. According to upguard.com, the Chroma abuse is a novel observation of the actor's TTPs and validates concerns about poorly secured AI technologies exposed to the internet.

Detection Recommendations

Require authentication and network isolation on all Chroma and other vector database endpoints, and never expose retrieval stores to the internet. Monitor retrieval stores for unexpected write activity and for injected ransom text in indexed documents. Track CVE-2025-3248 exposure on Langflow deployments and audit cloud service principals for unusual credential collection and destructive actions.
