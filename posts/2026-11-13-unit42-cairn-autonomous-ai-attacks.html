Detailed Findings

According to unit42.paloaltonetworks.com, from August to September 2026 Chinese speaking attackers used an open source AI penetration testing framework called Cairn to autonomously conduct 25 operations against at least 19 organizations. The attackers weaponized Cairn by pairing it with Chinese AI models Zhipu GLM and DeepSeek. The attackers supplied a target and a goal, then Cairn planned, executed, and recorded results on its own. These attacks achieved unauthorized access or data retrieval at 10 of the targeted organizations. Unit 42 reported access to dozens of valid credentials, mass exposure of personal data exceeding one million records at one organization, retrieval of military and government identities, creation of persistent administrator accounts in a production system, and modification of a live industry standards ballot document. Unit 42 assesses with moderate confidence that this activity represents China nexus intelligence collection tracked as CL-STA-1238. The article details targeting of advanced semiconductor manufacturing, U.S. defense policy, and civil society.
